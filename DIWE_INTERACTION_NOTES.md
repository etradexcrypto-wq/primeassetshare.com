# DIWE interaction reference

- The desktop header is a slim transparent navigation laid over the hero, with compact dropdown groups and a high-contrast CTA. Its visual weight stays low until the user interacts.
- The hero is asymmetric: compact copy and CTA on the left, a large human cutout with floating metric UI on the right, all over a saturated blurred color field.
- Content is presented through full-width visual chapters, not uniform card grids. Major images enter as oversized focal points with copy beside or over them.
- A tab rail sits at the bottom of the hero and changes the featured visual/story without reloading the page.
- Partner marks form a continuous horizontal trust strip.
- Solution/case cards use image-first compositions, bold saturated backgrounds, alternating image crops, and clear category/title hierarchy.
- Scroll entrances are restrained: opacity plus short vertical/side translation, followed by image scale settling. Horizontal motion is used for marquees and card rails.
- Cards rely on large artwork, clipped overflow, subtle hover zoom, and directional movement rather than heavy borders.
- The browser scroll tool could not move the current DIWE browser surface in this session; the live DOM, raw Webflow HTML, extracted assets, and full-page browser screenshots were used to verify the same patterns.
